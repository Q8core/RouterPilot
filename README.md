# RouterPilot — tune your home router for lower ping (Android)

**Free · Android 7+ · Arabic & English**

<img width="2750" height="900" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2705c5fd-0639-450e-8173-18808ef77579" />


Get a lower, steadier ping for **PUBG Mobile**, Free Fire, Call of Duty, FIFA and other online games, straight from your phone. Sign in with your router's admin page password (not the Wi-Fi password).

- **Game ping mode:** live ping while you play, with stats and a plain-language verdict on what is causing lag.
- **Best DNS finder:** tests Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, OpenDNS and others, and applies the fastest in one tap where the router allows it.
- **Signal optimiser:** finds the 4G/5G band combination with the lowest ping or highest speed (quick or deep test), with bands you can rule out. If a change is worse, it puts the old one back.
- **Network mode:** Auto, 4G only or 5G preferred. 4G only often lowers ping in competitive games.
- **Speed test** (quick or deep): download, upload and latency.
- **Game port profiles** for PUBG, Fortnite, CoD, FIFA, Apex, Valorant, LoL, Rocket League and GTA on PS5, Xbox and PC. Also UPnP and DMZ (with a clear warning).
- **Connected devices list**, a scheduler, and **Outage Detective**, which logs when your internet drops.
- **Works with** Huawei, ZTE, TP-Link, Fiberhome, OpenWrt and other routers. Some carriers lock certain settings on their routers, and the app tells you when that happens.
